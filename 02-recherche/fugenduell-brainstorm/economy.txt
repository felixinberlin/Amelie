Crack Flora Watcher: The Seed Market & Cross-City Trading
Version: 1.0
Status: Mechanics Proposal
Purpose: To define an in-game economy where players can sell, buy, and gift plant material, enabling cross-city battles while preserving scientific integrity.

1. Design Philosophy
The core tension: Real plants are bound to real places. But players want to use their plants everywhere.

The solution: Separate the plant from its seed.

The Individuum (real plant) stays in its Stelle. It cannot be traded. It is scientifically bound to its location.

The Samen (Seed) is an abstraction — a card that carries the plant's genetic profile, stats, skills, and lineage. It can be traded, gifted, and used in battles anywhere.

This is scientifically defensible: human-mediated seed dispersal is a real ecological mechanism. "Sohlenfracht" (shoe transport) is already a skill for Plantago major. The market models this phenomenon in-game while keeping the real observation record clean.

2. What Can Be Traded (and What Cannot)
Asset	Tradable?	Why
Individuum (real plant)	❌ No	Scientifically bound to its Stelle. Custody can change, but the plant stays.
Samen (seed card)	✅ Yes	Abstracted genetic material. Used in battles, not in real claims.
Steckling (cutting card)	✅ Yes	For vegetatively propagating species. Same logic as seeds.
Erstsichter credit	❌ No	Permanent. Cannot be sold or transferred.
Chronist custody	❌ No	Must be earned through care and defense. Cannot be bought.
Hüter role	❌ No	Social role. Must be earned.
Observations	❌ No	Scientific record. Permanent, attributed to the observer.
Stelle	❌ No	The place itself. Cannot be owned.
Key principle: You can trade the potential of a plant (its seeds), but not the plant itself or the scientific credit for it.

3. The Seed Card System
3.1 What Is a Seed Card?
A Samenkarte (seed card) is a digital record of a plant's genetic profile, extracted at Level 3 (Phenology). It contains:

Field	Description
Species	Scientific and common name
Stats	The 6 core stats, inherited from the parent plant
Skills	The parent's unlocked skills
CSR Class	Competitor, Stress-tolerator, Ruderal
Lineage	The chain of parent plants (Stammbaum)
Origin	The Stelle where the parent grew
Chronist	The player who harvested the seed
Level	The parent's level at harvest (Lv 3–5)
Signature Move	The parent's unlocked Signature Move
Mutation	A rare random variation (1–5% chance)
3.2 How to Harvest a Seed
Requires the parent plant to be at Level 3 (Phenology) or higher.

Harvesting costs Pollen (soft currency).

Each plant can produce 1 seed per season (4 per year).

The seed's stats are 95–105% of the parent's stats (slight variation).

A 5% chance of a Mutation — a random stat bonus (+1 to +3) or a rare skill variant.

3.3 Seed Rarity Tiers
Tier	Color	How to Get	Market Value
Gewöhnlich (Common)	Gray	Harvested from Lv 3 plants	Low
Selten (Uncommon)	Green	Harvested from Lv 4 plants	Medium
Episch (Epic)	Blue	Harvested from Lv 5 plants	High
Legendär (Legendary)	Gold	Mutation or Erstfund	Very High
Mythisch (Mythic)	Purple	5+ year lineage or extinct-in-city species	Extremely High
3.4 The Lineage Record (Stammbaum)
Every seed card carries its lineage: the chain of parent plants going back to the original Erstsichter.

text
Samenkarte #4821 — Taraxacum officinale
  ← harvested from Individuum #1203 (Berlin, Lv 5)
    ← harvested from Individuum #882 (Berlin, Lv 4)
      ← harvested from Individuum #341 (Berlin, Lv 3)
        ← Erstsichter: @anna_k (Berlin, 2024)
Why lineage matters:

A seed from a 5-year lineage is more valuable.

A seed from a famous Stelle or Chronist carries prestige.

A seed from a plant that survived an extreme event is historically significant.

The lineage is a real scientific record of human-mediated dispersal.

4. The Market
4.1 Market Types
Market	Description	Access
Auktionshaus (Auction House)	List a seed with a starting bid and duration (1h, 6h, 24h). Highest bidder wins.	Global
Sofortkauf (Buy Now)	Fixed-price listing. First buyer wins.	Global
Tausch (Direct Trade)	Player-to-player trade. Both must confirm.	Friends
Geschenk (Gift)	Free transfer. One per day per friend.	Friends
Stadtmarkt (City Market)	Local market. Only seeds from your city.	City
Samenbank (Seed Bank)	Deposit seeds for later use. Earns interest over time.	Personal
4.2 Listing a Seed
Select a seed from your inventory.

Choose market type (Auction, Buy Now, Trade, Gift).

Set price (in Pollen or Nektar).

Set duration (for auctions).

Confirm. The seed is escrowed until sold or expired.

Fee: 5% of the sale price is burned (removed from the economy). This prevents inflation.

4.3 Buying a Seed
Browse the market (filter by species, stats, price, city, lineage).

Place a bid or buy instantly.

The seed is transferred to your inventory.

The seller receives payment minus the 5% fee.

4.4 Cross-City Battles
This is the key feature: a player in Paris can use a Berlin seed to fight in Paris arenas.

Aspect	How It Works
Arena	The battle takes place in the local arena of the challenger.
Stats	The seed's stats are used as-is. No penalty for foreign origin.
CSR	The seed's CSR class applies normally.
Arena modifiers	The local arena modifiers apply. A Berlin dandelion in a Paris sidewalk crack uses Paris sidewalk modifiers.
Flavor	The battle log notes: "This Taraxacum officinale originates from Berlin, Kreuzberg. It fights in Paris, Marais."
Scientific record	The battle is logged as a simulated event. It does not create a real observation.
Why this works:

It creates a global seed economy.

It allows players to experiment with species from other climates.

It models human-mediated dispersal (a real ecological phenomenon).

It does not corrupt the scientific record.

6. Economy Balance
6.1 Preventing Pay-to-Win
Nektar cannot buy stats. It can only buy cosmetics, convenience, and rare seeds.

Rare seeds are not stronger. They are rarer, not better. A Legendary seed has the same stat budget as a Common seed. It just has a more interesting lineage.

Mutations are small. +1 to +3 to a single stat. Not game-breaking.

Skills are earned, not bought. A seed carries the parent's skills, but the buyer must still level it up to unlock them.

6.2 Preventing Market Manipulation
Price floors and ceilings: No seed can be listed below 10 Pollen or above 10,000 Pollen.

Cooldowns: One trade per player per hour.

Anti-bot: Observations must be verified (photo + GPS + AI confirmation) before seeds can be harvested.

Audit log: All trades are logged. Suspicious patterns are flagged.

6.3 Preventing Inflation
5% market fee burned. This removes Pollen from the economy.

Seed decay: Seeds in the Samenbank lose 1% stat value per month if not used. This encourages circulation.

Seasonal resets: At the end of each year, all seeds lose 10% of their market value. This prevents hoarding.

7. Scientific Value
The market is not just a game feature. It produces real scientific data:

Data Type	Source	Scientific Value
Human-mediated dispersal	Seed trades between cities	Tracks how humans move plant genetic material
Common garden experiment	Same species grown in different cities	Compares phenotypic plasticity across climates
Lineage records	Stammbaum chains	Tracks genetic lineages of urban plants
Market prices	Pollen/Nektar values	Proxy for perceived plant value (cultural ecology)
Rarity maps	Which species are rare where	Biogeographic data on urban flora
Crucially: The market data is separate from the observation data. The scientific record of real plants in real cracks remains clean. The market is a layer on top.

8. Risks and Safeguards
Risk	Safeguard
Invasive species spread	Banned species (Ailanthus) cannot be traded. Region-specific bans apply.
Pay-to-win	Nektar cannot buy stats. Seeds are sidegrades, not upgrades.
Market manipulation	Price floors, ceilings, cooldowns, audit logs.
Bot farming	Observations require GPS + photo + AI verification.
Loss of connection to real places	The Individuum is never traded. Only seeds. The real plant stays.
Scientific corruption	Market data is separate from observation data. Battles are simulated.
Player harassment	Block lists, report system, trade confirmation.
Economic collapse	5% burn fee, seasonal decay, seed decay.
9. Research Notes: Similar Game Economies
9.1 Pokémon (GTS, Wonder Trade)
Link: https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-video-games/pokemon-sword-and-pokemon-shield/

Mechanics:

Global Trade Station (GTS): List a Pokémon, request another. Asynchronous.

Wonder Trade: Trade a Pokémon for a random one. Surprise mechanic.

Link Trade: Direct player-to-player trade.

Relevance: The GTS is a model for our Auktionshaus. Wonder Trade is a model for a "seed lottery" feature. The separation of real plants (Individuen) and seeds mirrors the separation of Pokémon and eggs.

9.2 Neopets (Player Shops)
Link: https://www.neopets.com/

Mechanics:

Player-owned shops with customizable prices.

User-to-user trades with a "wishlist" system.

Auction house with bidding and buy-now options.

Relevance: The player shop model is a model for our Stadtmarkt (city market). The wishlist system is a model for our seed requests.

9.3 Animal Crossing (Turnip Market)
Link: https://www.animal-crossing.com/new-horizons/

Mechanics:

Stalk Market: Buy turnips on Sunday, sell during the week at fluctuating prices.

Nook's Cranny: Fixed-price shop.

Player-to-player trading: Via Dodo Codes.

Relevance: The turnip market is a model for seasonal seed pricing. The Dodo Code system is a model for direct trades.

9.4 Flight Rising (Auction House)
Link: https://www1.flightrising.com/

Mechanics:

Auction house with bids and buy-now.

Cross-realm trading.

Marketplace with fixed prices.

Coliseum (battles) drops items that feed the economy.

Relevance: The auction house is a direct model for our Auktionshaus. The cross-realm trading is a model for cross-city seed trading.

9.5 Warframe (Player Trading)
Link: https://www.warframe.com/

Mechanics:

Player-to-player trading via dojos.

Platinum (premium currency) as the primary trade currency.

Trade chat and third-party marketplaces (warframe.market).

Relevance: The premium currency as trade medium is a model for our Nektar. The third-party marketplace suggests we could allow API access for market data.

9.6 Axie Infinity (NFT Creatures)
Link: https://axieinfinity.com/

Mechanics:

Each Axie is a unique NFT with genetic traits.

Breeding creates new Axies with inherited traits.

Marketplace for buying, selling, and renting Axies.

Relevance: The breeding and inheritance system is a model for our seed lineage. The marketplace is a model for our seed market. The rental system is a model for our "gift" mechanic.

9.7 Gods Unchained (Card Trading)
Link: https://godsunchained.com/

Mechanics:

Cards are NFTs that can be traded.

Forge mechanic: burn duplicate cards to create higher-quality cards.

Marketplace with buy-now and auctions.

Relevance: The forge mechanic is a model for our mutation system. The card trading is a model for our seed cards.

9.8 Viridi (Plant Care)
Link: https://viridi.itch.io/viridi

Mechanics:

No trading. No market. Single-player plant care.

Relevance: The absence of an economy is notable. Viridi is purely about care. Crack Flora adds economy on top.

Cross-city battles	Use local arenas, foreign seeds. Simulated, not real.
Scientific value	Human-mediated dispersal, common garden, lineage records
Safeguards	Price floors/ceilings, 5% burn, seed decay, audit logs
Pay-to-win prevention	Nektar cannot buy stats. Seeds are sidegrades.
The core idea: You can trade the potential of a plant (its seeds), but not the plant itself or the scientific credit for it. This preserves the integrity of the citizen science data while enabling a rich, global economy.

A player in Paris can use a Berlin dandelion seed to fight in Paris arenas. The battle is simulated. The seed is a card. The real dandelion stays in Berlin, still growing, still being observed, still contributing to science.

That is the balance.

Signature Move (Lv 3)
⭐ AUSTRÖCKNUNGSSTARRE
When you lose a DÜRRE round, you fully recover in the next round. The plant dries to 4–7% relative water content, appears dead — and revives within 2–4 hours of rain. No coverage loss below 5% from drought events.

Why this matters: This is the mechanic that makes the Mauerraute viable. In a battle against a drought-adapted competitor, the Mauerraute cannot win the drought round outright. But it cannot be killed by drought, either. It simply waits.

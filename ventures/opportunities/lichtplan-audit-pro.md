## 1. The Core Commercial Problem
In Germany and across Europe, outdoor lighting and extensive glass facades are subject to increasingly strict environmental regulations (DIN EN 12464-2, Federal Immission Control Act / LAI-Hinweise, municipal skyglow statutes, and bird-friendly glass requirements like LAG VSW 21-01).
- **The Pain:** When architects submit building permits (*Bauantrag*), missing or non-compliant lighting/glazing documentation leads to project delays, rework, or rejected environmental assessments. Hiring external ecological lighting consultants costs €1,500–€5,000 per project.
- **The Solution:** An automated pre-flight audit tool for architects:
  1. Input luminaire specs (lumens, color temperature in Kelvin, beam angle, upward light output ratio ULOR, shielding) and facade glazing properties.
  2. The engine evaluates the project against municipal guidelines (e.g. Hamburg BUKEA, Berlin, Bayern) and DIN norms.
  3. Outputs a certified **Environmental Lighting & Bird Protection Compliance Report** (PDF) with exact citations, ready to append to the official *Bauantrag*.

## 1. The Core Commercial Problem
Under the EU Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (CSDDD) and CSRD European Sustainability Reporting Standard **ESRS E4 (Biodiversity and Ecosystems)**, over 50,000 European companies must report their material nature dependencies and ecological compensation balances starting 2025/2026.
- **The Pain:** While carbon accounting (Scope 1–3) is relatively standardized, biodiversity accounting (species richness, habitat integrity, soil health) is completely non-standardized. Companies spend €20,000–€80,000 on ESG consultants per reporting cycle.
- **The Current Alternative:** Manual Excel spreadsheets or complex enterprise ESG suites (Watershed, Sweep) that lack localized biodiversity GIS indicators and TNFD LEAP workflows.
- **The Solution:** A dedicated Nature Portfolio manager that treats ecological compensation plots (*Ökokonto-Maßnahmen* according to § 16 BNatSchG) like financial assets, tracking cumulative ecological ROI, species restoration indicators, and generating CSRD-compliant ESRS E4 disclosures.

## 3. Minimum Viable Product (MVP) Scope
1. **Compensation Asset Registry:** Ingest ecological plots, baseline biotope types, and target conservation values.
2. **TNFD LEAP Wizard:** Guided questionnaire establishing organizational nature dependencies.
3. **Copernicus Sentinel NDVI/Moisture Tracking:** Automated remote monitoring of biotope restoration progress.
4. **CSRD E4 Audit Export:** One-click export formatted according to EFRAG XBRL taxonomy for auditors.

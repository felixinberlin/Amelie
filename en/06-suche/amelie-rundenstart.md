## At the start of a round

1. **Read:** `amelie-suchplaybook.md` (method, atlas, latest retro) → `amelie-pruefprotokoll.md` → `amelie-quellen.md`.
2. **Apply the last retro:** do the "next time" item from the previous round first.
3. **Recheck what's due:** re-check every row in the log whose "recheck by" date has arrived, and every `unclear` row, first.
4. **Pick a source:** one `open` source from `amelie-quellen.md`, Type A or B preferred. Derive ideas from it, not the other way around.
5. **Pre-filter:** hold every idea against the occupied-territory atlas. If it lands in a `dense` field, don't search — log it directly as `occupied (atlas)`.

## During the round

- At most 4 searches per idea, order as in the playbook (recipient first).
- Record every verdict immediately with evidence, not from memory at the end.
- Target: 8–12 ideas checked. More than that is collecting, not checking.

## At the end of a round

1. **Log:** add a new round section with all verdicts.
2. **Sources:** update the status of the sources used.
3. **Playbook:**
   - Add to the hit-rate table.
   - Add newly discovered dense or open fields to the occupied-territory atlas.
   - Write the retro: *learned / mistake / next time*. At least one concrete "next time" item.
4. **Output:** all changed files in full, so Félix can upload them to the project.
5. **Loop check:** is there a ready-to-send tin sitting undelivered? If so, say so, once, briefly.

---

## How to tell the learning is working

| Signal | Good | Bad |
|---|---|---|
| "Open + narrowed" hit rate | rises over rounds | stays around 20% |
| Kills via the atlas instead of searching | increase | searches get wasted on the obvious |
| Repeated ideas | zero (the log catches it) | an idea shows up twice |
| "Next time" items | get done in the following round | pile up unread |

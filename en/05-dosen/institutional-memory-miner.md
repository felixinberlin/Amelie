# Institutional Memory Miner (IM Miner)

## Problem Statement

Public institutions, universities, and NGOs lose enormous amounts of critical knowledge every year due to staff turnover, project transitions, and inadequate documentation. This "institutional memory" – often in the form of experiential knowledge, historical contexts of decisions, unwritten protocols, or contact networks – is elusive and disappears when experienced staff leave or projects conclude. The consequences include inefficiency, repeated mistakes, longer onboarding times for new employees, and diminished organizational resilience. Current knowledge management systems are often too generic, reactive, or overwhelming with the sheer volume of information, instead of focusing on critical, at-risk knowledge.

## Proposed Solution: Asymmetric Inversion with IM Miner

The Institutional Memory Miner (IM Miner) is a proactive, structured knowledge capture tool designed to identify critical knowledge gaps and systematically distill the experiential knowledge of experts before it is lost. Instead of attempting to document *everything*, the IM Miner inverts the perspective: it asks *what knowledge is most critical* and *most at risk* of being lost, and then provides a guided process for effectively capturing precisely that knowledge.

### Core Features:

1.  **Knowledge Gap Identification:** Intuitive tools for teams and managers to define critical knowledge areas at risk (e.g., "How to manage specific grant funds?", "Historical context of a policy decision," "Troubleshooting legacy system X").
2.  **Guided Elicitation Templates:** Structured interview and questionnaire templates tailored to different knowledge types (e.g., project handover, succession planning, process documentation, lessons learned). These templates guide experts through the knowledge distillation process.
3.  **Modular Knowledge Units:** Knowledge is captured in small, linked, and semantically enriched units (so-called "knowledge artifacts"), e.g., "Decision Rationale," "Known Pitfalls," "Key Contacts," "Procedural Steps."
4.  **Expert Profiling & Matching:** The system helps identify internal experts and facilitates structured interviews or self-documentation sessions.
5.  **Version Control & Review:** Ensures that knowledge artifacts remain current, validated, and relevant.
6.  **Semantic Search & Discovery:** Enables easy retrieval of relevant knowledge content by topic, role, project, or keyword.
7.  **"Knowledge Sunset" Tracking:** Flagging knowledge units for periodic review or archiving to ensure continued relevance.

## Institutional Benefits

The IM Miner offers public institutions a range of advantages:

*   **Reduction of Friction:** Minimizes the effort required to retrieve lost knowledge and onboard new employees.
*   **Improved Decision-Making:** Ensures that historical contexts and best practices are readily accessible.
*   **Increased Resilience:** Protects against the loss of critical skills and information during personnel changes.
*   **Knowledge Equity:** Democratizes access to crucial institutional knowledge.
*   **Cost Savings:** Prevents the repetition of errors and inefficient processes.

## Technical Implementation

The tool can be developed as a web-based application with a focus on a user-friendly interface. Knowledge artifacts can be stored in Markdown or structured JSON to ensure flexibility and future extensibility (e.g., through LLM integration for template generation or summarization). The core functionality is based on an intelligent data model for knowledge gaps, expert profiles, knowledge artifacts, and elicitation templates.

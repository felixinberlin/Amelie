# Evidence Compass: Research Knowledge for Municipal Policy

## Problem Statement
Municipal administrations and civil society organizations face the challenge of making evidence-based decisions. The specialized knowledge required for this is often rooted in academic research, which is difficult to access, extensive, and challenging to assess for its relevance to specific local contexts. This creates a significant "Friction Gap" between scientific insight and practical application in municipal policy.

## Solution Idea: Evidence Compass
The Evidence Compass is an AI-powered tool designed to bridge this gap. It enables municipal actors to formulate specific local challenges (e.g., "strategies for reducing urban heat islands in the city center," "effective models for citizen participation in urban planning"). The tool then searches open-access research databases (e.g., arXiv, Zenodo, university repositories) and uses advanced NLP and LLM techniques to:
1. **Identify relevant research**: Filtering by topics, methods, and regional applicability.
2. **Extract actionable insights**: Sifting out study results, policy recommendations, and best practices directly tailored to the formulated challenge.
3. **Contextualize results**: Adapting findings to the user's specific local context (e.g., population density, climate zone, socioeconomic factors).
4. **Visualize and prepare**: Presenting key findings, potential interventions, and their evidence strength in an understandable format (e.g., interactive dashboards, policy briefs).

## Asymmetric Inversion
Instead of administrations laboriously commissioning their own research or painstakingly sifting through all scientific literature, the Evidence Compass *inverts* the process: It proactively and custom-delivers relevant scientific insights to decision-makers, thereby democratizing access to evidence-based foundations.

## Use Cases
- Urban planning offices seeking the latest findings on climate-resilient urban development.
- Environmental protection organizations requiring scientific evidence for their advocacy work.
- Social services departments researching effective programs for poverty reduction or social inclusion.

## Technological Basis
- **LLMs (Large Language Models)**: For semantic analysis, extraction, and synthesis of information.
- **NLP (Natural Language Processing)**: For topic identification and filtering.
- **Open-Access APIs**: For accessing scientific publications.
- **Web Framework**: For the user interface and data visualization.

The Evidence Compass aims to build a bridge between research and practice, thereby sustainably improving the quality of municipal decisions.

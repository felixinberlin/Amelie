# Entomo-Diagnostica: Invertebrate Bio-Indicator for Local Ecosystem Health

**Problem Statement:**
Rapid, cost-effective, and accessible assessment of local ecosystem health is often challenging. Traditional methods require specialized knowledge, specific equipment, and are difficult to scale for broad, continuous monitoring by non-specialists. This leads to delays in identifying environmental stressors and reduced public engagement in monitoring.

**Solution (Analogous Collision: Forensic Entomology + Citizen Science):**
"Entomo-Diagnostica" is a mobile-first, web-based tool that enables citizen scientists, students, or municipal workers to photograph and identify key invertebrate indicator species (e.g., aquatic insect larvae in streams, ground beetles in soil, pollinators). The tool uses a combination of image recognition (or guided identification) and a curated database of ecological indicator values (e.g., pollution tolerance indices, habitat requirements, trophic levels) to provide an immediate, localized "ecological health report" or "stress indicator" for the observed environment. This approach democratizes initial ecological assessment, transforming every nature observer into an "ecological detective."

**How it Works:**
1.  **Observation & Photo:** Users take a photo of an invertebrate species found in a specific habitat (e.g., streambed, soil sample).
2.  **Identification:** The tool attempts to identify the species via image recognition. If uncertain, it prompts for specific features (guided identification) or allows for community validation.
3.  **Contextualization:** Users provide basic information about the location (GPS, habitat type).
4.  **Diagnosis:** Based on the identified species and its stored ecological indicator values (e.g., saprobic index for water bodies, presence of heavy metal bioaccumulators), the tool generates an assessment of the ecological condition of the site.
5.  **Visualization:** Results are visualized and aggregated on a map to identify hotspots of environmental stress or particularly healthy areas.

**Target Institutions:**
BUND Berlin (German Federation for Environment and Nature Conservation), NABU (Nature And Biodiversity Conservation Union), local environmental agencies (e.g., Berlin Senate Department for Environment, Mobility, Consumer and Climate Protection), schools, universities (e.g., Freie Universität Berlin, Institute of Biology).

**Impact:**
*   **Early Detection:** Rapid identification of environmental problems (e.g., water pollution, habitat degradation).
*   **Citizen Participation:** Strengthens environmental awareness and active public involvement in nature conservation.
*   **Data Foundation:** Generates valuable, georeferenced data for science and administration.
*   **Education:** Serves as a practical teaching tool for schools and universities.

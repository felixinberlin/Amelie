# ProcureLens: Transparent Tender Analysis with AI

## Problem Statement
Public tenders and procurement processes are often complex, extensive, and written in legalistic jargon. This significantly hinders citizens, NGOs, journalists, and smaller businesses from understanding, monitoring, or actively participating in these processes. A lack of transparency can lead to inefficiencies, loss of trust, and failure to achieve policy goals (e.g., sustainability, local value creation). Manually analyzing large volumes of tender documents is time-consuming and resource-intensive.

## The Amélie Idea: ProcureLens
ProcureLens is an open-source tool that uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) to analyze public tender documents, making them accessible to citizens and civil society organizations. Its aim is to increase transparency in procurement processes and facilitate the monitoring of public spending.

## Core Features
1.  **Document Upload & Processing:** Allows uploading of PDF or text documents of public tenders.
2.  **Keyword and Theme Extraction:** Identifies relevant terms and themes such as sustainability criteria, social standards, local references, exclusion criteria, and contract terms.
3.  **Anomaly Detection:** Highlights unusual phrasing, potential conflicts of interest, or deviations from standard processes (e.g., unusually short deadlines, specific product specifications that might point to a single supplier).
4.  **Comparison Function:** Enables the comparison of multiple tenders or the analysis of changes over time.
5.  **Summary & Visualization:** Generates understandable summaries of key points and visualizes relevant data (e.g., procurement phases, bidder profiles).
6.  **Questions & Answers (Q&A):** A chat function allowing users to ask natural language questions about the documents (e.g., "What sustainability criteria are required?", "Are there clauses for subcontractor liability?").

## Technological Approach
ProcureLens utilizes modern Large Language Models (LLMs) and Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques for semantic text analysis. It relies on open-source models to ensure transparency and traceability of the analysis process. A web interface provides easy access and interaction.

## Target Audience
*   **Citizens:** Interested in the use of public funds.
*   **NGOs and Civil Society Organizations:** Promoting transparency and accountability in public administration.
*   **Journalists:** For investigative research in public procurement.
*   **Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs):** To better understand tenders and lower entry barriers.

## Potential Added Value
*   **Increased Transparency:** Opens complex processes to the public.
*   **Improved Accountability:** Enables better monitoring and identification of risks.
*   **Promotion of Fair Competition:** By uncovering potentially discriminatory clauses.
*   **Support for Strategic Goals:** Helps verify whether political objectives (e.g., climate protection, social justice) are actually implemented in procurement processes.
*   **Strengthening Civil Society:** Provides tools for effective civic engagement.

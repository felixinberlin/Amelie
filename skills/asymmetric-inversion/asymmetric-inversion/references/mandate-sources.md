# Mining Regulatory Mandates, Standards & Administrative Catalogs

How to find grounded Class A and Class B anchor sources for the Asymmetric Inversion Protocol.

---

## 1. Primary Source Catalogs (Type A & B Documents)

An inversion is only as strong as the grounding of its anchor system. Never invert an invented problem. Invert a published standard, an enacted regulation, or a printed fee schedule.

### Category 1: Technical Standards & Norms (DIN, EN, ISO, VDI)
Technical standards define quantitative thresholds that separate legal compliance from civil liability.
* **DIN / EN Standards**:
  - `DIN 4109` (Sound insulation in buildings / Schallschutz im Hochbau)
  - `DIN 18040` (Barrier-free construction / Barrierefreies Bauen)
  - `DIN EN 12464` (Lighting of indoor/outdoor workplaces / Beleuchtung)
  - `DIN 1986-100` (Drainage systems for buildings and property / Entwässerungsanlagen)
* **VDI Guidelines (Verein Deutscher Ingenieure)**:
  - `VDI 2055` (Thermal insulation for industrial installations)
  - `VDI 3787` (Environmental meteorology / Climate maps for cities)
* **Where to find them**:
  - University libraries (Perinorm / Nautos database access).
  - Public explanatory leaflets by trade associations (*Zentralverband des Deutschen Handwerks*, *BDEW*, *Fachverband Sanitär Heizung Klima*).
  - Search recipe: `<Normnummer> Leitfaden Handwerk PDF` or `<Norm> Auslegungshilfe Baupraxis`.

### Category 2: Municipal Statutes & Decrees (*Satzungsrecht*)
Municipal law is where national environmental policy meets local enforcement reality. German cities publish hundreds of local ordinances:
* **Key Municipal Ordinances**:
  - **Baumschutzsatzung**: Protection of urban trees, criteria for required replacement plantings, canopy diameter thresholds.
  - **Erhaltungssatzung / Milieuschutz**: Protection of neighborhood composition and architectural heritage.
  - **Abfallwirtschaftssatzung**: Regulations on bulky waste (*Sperrmüll*), hazardous materials, recycling bins.
  - **Straßenreinigungs- und Winterdienstsatzung**: Snow clearing duties, leaf removal, pedestrian safety.
  - **Entwässerungs- / Abwassersatzung**: Rainwater uncoupling fees, sealed surface assessments (*Niederschlagswassergebühr*).
* **Where to find them**:
  - Municipal portals (`berlin.de/ba-*-ordnungsamt/`, `ortsrecht.<stadt>.de`).
  - Search recipe: `<Stadt> Satzung über den Schutz des Baumbestandes PDF` · `<Stadt> Niederschlagswassergebühr Satzung Selbstauskunft`.

### Category 3: Fee Schedules (*Gebührenordnungen & Entgelttabellen*)
As discovered in Amélie Round 8, a fee schedule is not financial paperwork—it is an explicit **incentive map**.
* By comparing fee ladders, you can spot the structural failure points of an urban system:
  - e.g., Cost to dispose of a sofa legally via express pickup (€100) vs. with a car at a recycling depot (free) vs. dumping it at night on the curb (€0 to the dumper, €65 to the city taxpayer).
* **Where to find them**:
  - Municipal utility price lists: BSR, Berliner Wasserbetriebe, Stadtreinigung Hamburg, AWB Köln.
  - Search recipe: `<Entsorgungsbetrieb> Gebührentabelle PDF <Jahr>` · `<Kommunalbetrieb> Entgeltordnung`.

### Category 4: EU Directives with Upcoming National Enforcement
The European Union passes sweeping environmental and consumer directives that member states must transpose into national law over 2–4 years. These create massive, predictable tooling vacuums:
* **Current High-Impact EU Directives (2024–2028)**:
  - **ESPR (Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation)**: Digital Product Passports (DPP) for textiles, batteries, electronics.
  - **EPBD (Energy Performance of Buildings Directive Recast)**: Mandatory zero-emission standards, minimum energy performance standards (MEPS).
  - **Nature Restoration Law (Wiederherstellung der Natur)**: Binding restoration targets for agricultural ecosystems, urban green spaces, river connectivity.
  - **Right to Repair Directive (Recht auf Reparatur)**: Access to spare parts, schematics, and independent diagnostic standards.
  - **EU Soil Monitoring Law**: Monitoring soil health, sealing rates, and contamination indicators.
* **Where to find them**:
  - EUR-Lex (`eur-lex.europa.eu`).
  - BMUV / BMWK legislative monitors (`bmuv.de/gesetzgebung`).
  - Search recipe: `Richtlinie <Name> Umsetzung Frist Deutschland` · `<EU-Richtlinie> Anforderungen Handwerk KMU`.

## 2. The Extraction Recipe: From Legal Text to Inversion Anchor

When reading a mandate or norm document, extract exactly four elements:

1. **The Threshold Value ($\theta$)**: What exact number triggers legal consequence? (e.g. $> 12$ cm trunk diameter, $> 45\ \text{dB(A)}$ at night, $> 4$ bird collision casualties per 100m facade).
2. **The Prescribed Verification Method**: How does the law say it must be proved? (e.g. self-disclosure questionnaire, specialized engineering report, photometric file, logbook).
3. **The Unfunded Party**: Who is legally obligated to provide this proof, but lacks a dedicated software budget? (tenants, building owners, small businesses, tree wardens, citizen monitors).
4. **The Sensor / Multimodal Substitute**: What commodity edge capability can capture the required proof without certified hardware? (microphone calibration against a known reference, photogrammetry with a reference card, smartphone LiDAR, audio spectral analysis).

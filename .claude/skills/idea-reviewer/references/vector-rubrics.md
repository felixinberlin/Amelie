# The 7-Vector Scoring Rubrics

Detailed quantitative scoring standards, calibration anchors, and deduction rules for the Idea Reviewer.

---

## Vector 1 · Novelty & Distance

Measures conceptual distance from the collective LLM mode, commercial startup solutions, and established open source.

| Score | Classification | Definition | Calibration Anchor |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Mode Collapse** | Direct recombination of standard consumer concepts ("Tinder for X", "Notion for Y", generic habit tracker). Exists in hundreds of app store copies. | *Mängelanzeige-Assistent für Mieter* (Killed in R1, dozens of VC clones) |
| **2** | **Combinational** | Combines two familiar tools in the same problem space. Easily generated by zero-shot LLM prompts. | *Gamifizierte Ritzenpflanzen-Entdeckung* (Killed in R2, Seek/Flora Incognita already did it) |
| **3** | **Exploratory** | Extends an established standard, guideline, or dataset into a previously unautomated niche. Passes the Lacunar Gap test. | *Glasanflug-Ampel* (Automates LAG-VSW 21/01 norm via phone camera reflections) |
| **4** | **Asymmetric Inversion** | Flips an industrial/regulatory compliance pipeline into a citizen evidentiary shield. Unmonetizable by SaaS. | *Hof-Entkoppler* (DWA-A 138-1 percolation timer $\to$ municipal fee waiver) |
| **5** | **Transformational** | Establishes a new capability rule space or defensive protocol that renders existing expensive workflows obsolete. | *Altbau-Thermal* (2-brick thermal simulation without €2,000 consultant report) |

### Deduction Rules
* Deduct **-2 points** if the gap cannot be stated without naming the technical solution.
* Deduct **-1 point** if a VC-funded startup launched a direct commercial equivalent within the past 18 months.

## Vector 2 · Technical Complexity & Execution Burden

Measures implementation barrier, operational maintenance, and alignment with Amélie's Rule 4 (gifts must not burden solo recipients).

| Score | Complexity Tier | Architecture & Runtime | Maintenance Risk |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Minimal Static** | Single HTML/TS file, pure DOM/Canvas, deterministic formula calculation, zero dependencies. | Zero maintenance. Runs forever on GitHub Pages. |
| **2** | **Client-Side Heavy** | WebGL / WebGPU shaders, Web Audio API, or WASM runtime (e.g. SQLite WASM, ONNX Runtime Web). Zero server. | Very low. Dependent only on modern browser standards. |
| **3** | **Static Open Data Hybrid** | Large pre-indexed open datasets served as static tiles (PMTiles, GeoJSON, COG) via CDN. Client-side spatial queries. | Low. Periodic static file updates (e.g. annual municipal geodata). |
| **4** | **Ephemeral API Bridge** | Requires live public CORS endpoints or ephemeral proxy workers. Stateless. | Moderate. Public APIs may change auth or rate limits. |
| **5** | **Stateful Distributed Server** | Requires continuous database (PostgreSQL), background daemon workers, user accounts, and authenticated microservices. | **Severe Red Flag**. Solo maintainers cannot afford to host or maintain. |

### Scoring Inversion Note
For Amélie, **lower technical complexity is preferred**. A Tier 1 or Tier 2 architecture receives a high fitness score (5/5), whereas a Tier 5 architecture receives 1/5.

## Vector 3 · Possibility & "Why Now" Reality Check

Measures feasibility against physics, acoustic/optical signal-to-noise ratios, edge hardware, and legal boundaries.

| Score | Feasibility State | Physical / Legal Evidence | "Why Now" Catalyst |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Physical Impossibility** | Signal-to-noise ratio is too low for commodity hardware (e.g. measuring pipe wall thickness through plaster via phone ultrasound). | None. Pure wishful thinking. |
| **2** | **Cloud-Dependent / Latency Blocked** | Requires 10-second roundtrips to 70B parameter cloud models, incurring recurring API costs. | Pre-2024 cloud API wrapping. Fails Rule 4. |
| **3** | **Marginally Viable** | Feasible under strict conditions (good lighting, quiet room, modern iPhone), but prone to edge failures in amateur hands. | General edge compute availability. |
| **4** | **Solid Edge Reality** | Physically grounded in robust commodity sensors (macro camera, audio FFT resonance, magnet, WebGL). Clean GDPR status. | Post-2024 on-device multimodal models or WebGPU compute. |
| **5** | **Bulletproof Grounding** | Deterministic physical or legal mapping with zero room for model hallucinations (e.g. DIN mathematical formulas, lookup tables). | New statutory deadline (e.g. TrinkwV 12.01.2026) + WebAssembly. |

## Vector 4 · Future Runway, Policy Alignment & Longevity

Measures the durability of the gift and how long it remains relevant to civil society.

| Score | Longevity Profile | Regulatory / Societal Alignment | Recipient Half-Life |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Ephemeral Trend** | Tied to a short-lived social media trend, temporary grant buzzword, or dying platform. | Obsolete in $< 6$ months. |
| **2** | **Volatile Environment** | Solves an issue in a rapidly shifting commercial ecosystem where big tech regularly deprecates APIs. | 12 months before major breakage. |
| **3** | **Stable Civic Need** | Addresses a permanent local community need (repair cafes, community gardens, local tenant complaints). | 2–3 years with minimal maintenance. |
| **4** | **Statutory Mandate (National)** | Directly tied to an enacted national law or technical norm (e.g. DIN 4109, TrinkwV, BWB Satzung). | 5–10 years (until the national norm is rewritten). |
| **5** | **EU Directive / Structural Law** | Aligned with binding European Union directives undergoing member-state transposition (ESPR, EPBD, Nature Restoration). | 10+ years. High-conviction institutional longevity. |

## Vector 5 · Grounded Civic SWOT Analysis

Evaluates the strategic fitness of the idea as an unconditional public-good gift.

* **Strengths (S)**:
  * 100% open-source CC0 public domain (defense against patenting).
  * No user accounts or PII storage (exempt from GDPR compliance overhead).
  * Immediate utility: Generates actionable output in $\le 3$ clicks.
* **Weaknesses (W)**:
  * Non-destructive / non-invasive testing cannot replace certified laboratory destruction tests.
  * Dependency on user diligence (e.g. user must perform scratch test properly).
* **Opportunities (O)**:
  * Integration into municipal platforms (CityLAB, Smart City initiatives).
  * Funding via Prototype Fund, Sovereign Tech Fund, or DBU.
  * Direct adoption by NGOs (NABU, vzbv, Deutscher Mieterbund).
* **Threats (T)**:
  * Legal threats / cease-and-desist from trade associations or property owner lobbies.
  * Fast-following commercial app stores cloning the concept and burying it under SEO ad spend.

## Vector 6 · Tech Tree Position & Capability Dependency Graph

Maps the idea to open-source scaffolding levels.

```
Level 0: Raw Open Standards & Datasets
         (DIN / EU Norms, KOSTRA-DWD, Overpass OSM, Web Standards)
                 │
Level 1: Reusable Edge Compute Kernels
         (WASM Tesseract, Web Audio FFT, WebGPU Compute Shaders, PMTiles)
                 │
Level 2: The Core Tool Scaffolding (Amélie First Step Ticket)
         (Interactive Diagnostic, Sizing Engine, Form Generator)
                 │
Level 3: Civic & Scientific Downstream Unlocks
         (Aggregated Citizen Science, Municipal Policy Dashboards, Research Validation)
```

* **Score 1–2**: Trapped at Level 0 (no viable edge kernel exists) or Level 3 (trying to build a massive ecosystem platform without a working Level 2 tool).
* **Score 3–5**: Perfectly positioned at Level 2, grounded on robust Level 0/1 primitives, unlocking concrete Level 3 civic power.

## Vector 7 · Available Documentation & Ground Truth Fidelity

Measures primary source grounding to ensure the idea is not a hallucinated need.

| Score | Source Class | Evidence Standard | Verification Status |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Hearsay / Blog** | Idea based on Reddit threads, Medium think-pieces, or general LLM impressions. | Unverified. High risk of false premise. |
| **2** | **Secondary News** | Newspaper article discussing a problem without linking legislation or technical metrics. | Anecdotal. Specific numbers absent. |
| **3** | **Type C / D Paper** | Peer-reviewed academic paper (arXiv, Springer) or documented AI pilot workshop report (CompGen). | Scientifically proven, but user friction unquantified. |
| **4** | **Type B Bottleneck** | Official NGO / research institute annual report explicitly naming an unautomated evaluation bottleneck (Thünen MonViA). | Operationally verified manual pain point. |
| **5** | **Type A Mandate** | Published legal ordinance (BGBl.), DIN/EN/VDI standard, or official municipal fee schedule (*Satzung*). | Absolute statutory ground truth. |

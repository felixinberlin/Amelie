### Next experiment — fully withheld-station feasibility

This is a proposed experiment, not an executed result or a new discovery.

1. Choose three compatible stations and one historical month before inspecting evaluation outcomes. Reuse Aloft/getRad; cap downloads at three monthly files / 500 MB. Record URLs, versions, hashes, license, UTC, height datum, station history and quality rules. Stop at incompatible units/heights/flags or insufficient overlap.
2. Hold one target station out of all training, tuning and threshold estimation. Use only the two donor stations' first 20 days to construct a time-of-night baseline and freeze parameters. Call it a donor-period baseline, not seasonal climatology: one month does not establish seasonal behavior. Evaluate complete held-out nights in the remaining period, using target observations only as evaluation truth.
3. Compare that baseline with distance-weighted donor reconstruction at matching timestamps/heights. This tests retrospective gap reconstruction, not forecasting. Define high-intensity thresholds from donor training data only. Rotate the withheld station with rules unchanged and report each station separately.
4. Measure MAE by height, per-night errors, high-intensity precision/recall and valid coverage; use night-block uncertainty intervals. Provisional continuation gate: at least 10% MAE reduction, recall at least 80%, precision at least 70%, five evaluation episodes and at least 80% valid coverage in every claimed height band. Freeze these research triage criteria in the manifest; they are not validated conservation thresholds. Report sensitivity and small sample size; never relax a failed gate after seeing results.
5. Stop for inadequate events/coverage or a failed gate; report insufficient evidence where appropriate. Budget: one working day, at most two CPU hours, no GPU or paid providers. Ask whether the resulting error/coverage report solves a real research workflow before proposing another Dose. No bird-mortality or operational-curtailment claim follows.

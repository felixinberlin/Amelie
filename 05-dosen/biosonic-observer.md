# Dossier: BioSonic Observer: Browser-Native Acoustic Biodiversity Scout

## Project Idea
The BioSonic Observer is a browser-based, locally running tool that allows citizens to upload audio recordings of their environment (e.g., bird songs, insect sounds) and receive real-time identification of potential species. By using WebGPU for AI inference directly in the browser and an agentic micro-harness for user guidance, the tool offers immediate, playful feedback and structures the collected data for later use by environmental organizations and municipalities.

## Problem Statement
Biodiversity monitoring is critical but often resource-intensive. Citizen science approaches are promising but often hampered by complex data submission processes and a lack of immediate feedback, which reduces participant motivation. Environmental agencies and NGOs need validated data to plan conservation measures but have limited capacity for manual audio data analysis.

## Amélie Approach
The project leverages current developments in local AI (WebGPU, WASM) and VibeCoding (delightful UX, zero-latency feedback) to create an accessible and motivating tool. The focus is on direct applicability and strengthening civic participation. It avoids generic dashboards or wrappers, offering instead a specific, playful solution to a real environmental problem.

## Target Institution
BUND (Bund für Umwelt und Naturschutz Deutschland) and the Berlin Senate Department for Environment, Mobility, Consumer and Climate Protection are ideal partners, as they are actively engaged in biodiversity monitoring and citizen participation. The tool could complement their existing initiatives and revolutionize data collection.

## Core Features
*   **Browser-Native Audio Analysis:** Upload or direct recording of environmental sounds within the browser.
*   **Real-time Species Identification:** Immediate display of potential bird, insect, or bat species through local AI models.
*   **Agentic Micro-Harness:** Guides users through the recording process, provides quality tips, and suggests local species.
*   **Gamification & Feedback:** Visually appealing presentation of results and progress to boost motivation.
*   **Structured Data Exports:** Easy export of identified species and metadata for NGOs and authorities (e.g., in formats for GBIF or iNaturalist).

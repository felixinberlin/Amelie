# Dose: Kiez-Pinsel (Neighborhood Brush)
Kiez-Pinsel is a browser-native tool that empowers citizens to provide visual feedback on urban public spaces. Users can upload photos of their neighborhood or use map views as a base, then utilize simple drawing tools and AI-generated "stickers" (e.g., trees, benches, bike racks) to directly visualize their ideas for changes. An integrated, local AI (WASM/WebGPU) identifies existing elements and assists with placing new ones. A small LLM translates the visual adjustments and short text prompts into structured, actionable feedback for urban planners. The goal is to lower the barrier for civic engagement in urban development and improve communication between citizens and administration by delivering concrete, visual proposals.

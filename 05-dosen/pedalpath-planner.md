# PedalPath Planner: WebGPU-Simulated Bicycle Infrastructure Designer

**Problem:** Current urban planning tools for bicycle infrastructure are often complex, static, and lack interactive capabilities for 'what-if' scenarios or public engagement. This leads to protracted processes and suboptimal designs.

**Amélie Solution:** A browser-native, WebGPU/WASM-powered tool that allows urban planners, ADFC members, or interested citizens to design proposed bicycle lanes, intersections, and other elements directly on a map. Micro-agents (simulated cyclists) navigate the proposed network in real-time, visualizing traffic flow, conflict points, and safety scores. The tool provides immediate visual feedback on design changes, akin to an urban 'tycoon' game.

**Technology:** Leverages WebGPU and WebAssembly for high-performance, client-side simulation and rendering. Agent-based models simulate the behavior of cyclists and other road users. Focus on intuitive UI and playful interaction.

**Benefit:** Accelerates planning processes, improves data-driven decision-making, and enhances citizen participation through tangible visualizations. Fosters safer and more efficient cycling infrastructure.

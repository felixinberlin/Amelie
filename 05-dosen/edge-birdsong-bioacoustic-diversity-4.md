# Dose: Edge-WASM Audio Feature Extractor for Vernal Forest Bird Diversity
**Domäne:** physics (CC0 1.0 Universal)
**Zielinstitution:** Museum für Naturkunde Berlin & Max Planck Institute of Animal Behavior

## Problemstellung & Evidenz
Runs zero-latency acoustic bio-index extraction (NDSI/ADI) on client devices without sending audio files to centralized cloud servers.

## Hypothese
Ecologists collect terabytes of field audio in remote nature reserves without cellular connectivity; client-side browser analysis enables real-time field validation.

## Architektur
Reine Client-seitige Zero-Drift Micro-Engine für öffentliche Verwaltungen und Wissenschaft.

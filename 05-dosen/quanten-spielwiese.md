# Quantum Playground: Browser-Native Quantum Mechanics Simulator with WebGPU

### Problem Statement
Quantum mechanics, a cornerstone of modern physics, often proves challenging for students due to its abstract nature. Traditional teaching methods and available simulation tools are frequently proprietary, technically outdated, or demand specific software installations, hindering interactive exploration and deeper understanding. There's a clear lack of freely available, high-performance, and visually engaging browser-based tools that facilitate playful engagement with quantum mechanical phenomena.

### Amélie Solution: Quantum Playground
The "Quantum Playground" is an interactive, browser-native simulator for fundamental quantum mechanics experiments. Leveraging WebGPU for real-time visualization of complex wave functions and probability distributions, and WebAssembly (WASM) for efficient numerical solutions of the Schrödinger equation, the playground enables intuitive exploration of phenomena such as particle in a box, tunneling effects, or the quantum harmonic oscillator. Users can dynamically adjust parameters and observe the immediate effects on the quantum mechanical system. An integrated "guided discovery" agent could also suggest interesting scenarios or provide explanations for observed phenomena.

### Benefits
*   **Accessibility:** Runs directly in the browser without installations, making quantum mechanics accessible to everyone.
*   **Engagement:** Highly interactive and visually appealing interface promotes playful learning and boosts motivation.
*   **Performance:** Utilizes modern web technologies (WebGPU, WASM) for fluid real-time simulations and complex visualizations.
*   **Educational Value:** Enhances conceptual understanding of abstract quantum phenomena through direct manipulation and observation.

### Use Cases
University education (Physics, Engineering), high school education (advanced physics courses), self-study, and popular science communication. Ideal for interactive lectures and exercises.

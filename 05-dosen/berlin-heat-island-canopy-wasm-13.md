# Dose: Copernicus LiDAR Canopy Thermal Walking Corridor Engine
**Domäne:** environment (CC0 1.0 Universal)
**Zielinstitution:** Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research (PIK) & Senatsverwaltung Berlin (Grünflächenamt)

## Problemstellung & Evidenz
Fuses Berlin LiDAR open tree point clouds with Copernicus Land Surface Temperature to generate 100% browser-WASM shaded transit corridors.

## Hypothese
Elderly pedestrian heatstroke hospitalizations spike 41% along bus routes devoid of tree canopy coverage.

## Architektur
Reine Client-seitige Zero-Drift Micro-Engine für öffentliche Verwaltungen und Wissenschaft.

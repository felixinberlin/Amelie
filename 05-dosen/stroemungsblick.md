# Dose: Strömungsblick: Urban Waterway Flow & Dispersion Modeler
**Domäne:** physics (CC0 1.0 Universal)
**Zielinstitution:** BUND Berlin / Berliner Wasserbetriebe / TU Berlin Open Science Lab

## Problemstellung & Evidenz
Municipalities and environmental NGOs often rely on expensive, proprietary software for urban waterway modeling, leading to opaque decision-making and limited public participation in water quality and flood risk management. Open-source alternatives are scarce for non-expert users.

## Hypothese
The lack of accessible, open-source tools for simulating urban waterway hydrodynamics and pollutant dispersion creates a civic friction, hindering public scrutiny, collaborative planning, and proactive environmental management by NGOs and smaller municipalities. Providing such a tool will democratize critical environmental insights.

## Architektur
Reine Client-seitige Zero-Drift Micro-Engine für öffentliche Verwaltungen und Wissenschaft.

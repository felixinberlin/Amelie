# Dose: School Gate Particulate & Acoustic Micro-Zone Sentry
**Domäne:** civic (CC0 1.0 Universal)
**Zielinstitution:** German Federal Environment Agency (UBA) & Parents For Future Berlin

## Problemstellung & Evidenz
Combines Sensor.Community crowd hardware with Berlin primary school bell schedules to trigger dynamic low-emission pedestrian school streets.

## Hypothese
Peak soot and ultrafine nanoparticle exposures occur precisely during the 18-minute morning student drop-off window directly at school gates.

## Architektur
Reine Client-seitige Zero-Drift Micro-Engine für öffentliche Verwaltungen und Wissenschaft.

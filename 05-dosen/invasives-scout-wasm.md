# Dossier: Browser-Native Invasive Species Scout

**Problem:** Invasive species pose a growing threat to local ecosystems. Early detection and reporting are crucial but often hampered by a lack of expert knowledge or slow, server-based identification processes.

**Solution:** A browser-based tool that leverages WebGPU/WASM-powered AI for instant, local identification of invasive plant and animal species. Users can upload or take photos directly in the browser and receive real-time assessments. Identified findings can then be geo-tagged and reported directly to environmental agencies or conservation organizations. The focus is on a playful, responsive user experience that also works offline.

**Amélie Fit:** Utilizes cutting-edge browser AI technologies (WASM/WebGPU) for a fast, privacy-preserving solution. Promotes citizen science and supports governmental tasks in nature conservation.

# Quantum Field Dynamics Visualizer (Browser-Native with AI-Perturbation)

## Problem Statement
Visualizing and interactively experimenting with complex concepts of Quantum Field Theory (QFT) is a significant challenge for students and researchers. Existing tools are often proprietary, require specialized hardware, or are static, hindering deep, intuitive understanding and rapid hypothesis testing.

## Amélie Solution
A browser-native tool leveraging WebGPU for real-time 3D rendering of quantum field dynamics (e.g., virtual particles, wave functions, interaction vertices) and WASM for executing lightweight physics simulations. An integrated, small, browser-native AI model allows users to interactively 'perturb' the field (e.g., introduce a virtual particle, change a coupling constant) and instantly visualize the propagated effects. This promotes playful exploration and hypothesis generation.

## Target Audience
Physics departments at universities (e.g., Humboldt Universität zu Berlin), Max Planck Institutes for Physics, DESY (Deutsches Elektronen-Synchrotron), and science centers for public science communication.

## Added Value
Enhances physics education, promotes scientific literacy, provides an accessible tool for early-stage conceptual exploration in research without requiring expensive workstations or specialized software licenses.

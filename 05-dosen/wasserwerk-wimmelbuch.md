# Wasserwerk-Wimmelbuch: Interactive Water Cycle Explorer

## Problem Statement
The critical infrastructure of urban water supply and sanitation remains largely invisible and incomprehensible to most citizens. This leads to a lack of appreciation and understanding of the complex processes that secure our drinking water and purify wastewater. Existing information is often text-heavy, technical, or unappealing as static infographics.

## The Amélie Solution: An Interactive 'Busy Book'
The "Wasserwerk-Wimmelbuch" (Waterworks Busy Book) is a playful, highly detailed, and interactive web application that depicts the urban water cycle – from extraction, treatment, and distribution to wastewater disposal and purification – as a massive, animated 'busy book' illustration. Users can zoom into different scenes (e.g., a water treatment plant, a wastewater treatment plant, the sewage system under the city) and click on individual elements.

### Technology and Interaction
- **Visual Basis:** High-resolution, interactive vector graphics (SVG), optimized for fluid zooming and panning using WebGPU/WASM.
- **Interactive Elements:** Each clickable element (e.g., a filter basin, a pump, a pipe section, a measuring station, a microorganism in the wastewater treatment plant) opens a small information box. These boxes are populated with context-specific, easy-to-understand explanations generated by a client-side, lightweight LLM agent (micro-harness) or a knowledge base.
- **Dynamic Paths:** An AI-powered function allows users to ask questions like "Where does my drinking water come from?" or "How is wastewater purified?", upon which the agent dynamically highlights the water's path through the busy book illustration and explains the relevant stations.
- **Mini-Games:** Integration of small "find the element" games or quizzes to increase engagement and reinforce learning.

## Target Institution & Benefit
Berliner Wasserbetriebe (BWB) could use this tool to revolutionize public education about their infrastructure. It would be an ideal tool for schools, exhibition spaces, online communication, and citizen dialogues. The Berlin Senate Department for Environment could use it as a model for further environmental education projects.

## Civic Impact
- **Citizen Engagement:** Increases understanding and appreciation for vital public services.
- **Environmental Awareness:** Promotes awareness of the water cycle and the importance of water protection.
- **Transparency:** Makes complex technical processes transparent and accessible.
- **Education:** Provides an innovative learning platform for children and adults.

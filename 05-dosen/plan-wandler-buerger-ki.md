# Dossier: PlanWandler: Citizen AI for Urban Development Plans

**Problem:** Public participation in urban development planning is legally mandated in Germany but often ineffective. Zoning plans (Bebauungspläne) and other urban planning documents are extremely complex for laypersons, filled with jargon, and difficult to grasp. This leads to low citizen engagement or feedback that is challenging for authorities to process, as it is often unstructured or emotional. The result is an asymmetry between administration and citizens, causing frustration on both sides and diminishing the quality of urban development.

**Solution (Asymmetric Inversion):** PlanWandler is a browser-based, locally running AI tool (WebGPU/WASM) that helps citizens understand complex urban development plans and related documents. It offers:
1.  **Understandable Summaries:** Reduces bureaucratic texts to key points in plain language.
2.  **Jargon Decoder:** Explains technical terms and legal passages context-sensitively.
3.  **Impact Simulation (Text-based):** Based on the document's text, it simulates potential impacts of plan changes (e.g., "What does designating this area as 'commercial' mean for noise pollution or traffic?").
4.  **Structured Feedback Assistance:** Guides users in formulating well-founded and policy-relevant statements by identifying relevant sections and offering argumentation aids.

**Advantage (Friction & Enforcement Gap):** PlanWandler bridges the information gap between administration and citizens. It transforms passive, opaque documents into interactive, understandable tools. The "Enforcement Gap" – the difficulty in *effectively* exercising the legally enshrined right to participation – is closed by a proactive, citizen-friendly interface. It enables more informed and broader participation, leading to better planning outcomes and higher acceptance. The tool runs locally in the browser to ensure data privacy and independence.

# Lichtspielplatz: Browser-native Optics Simulation with WebGPU

## Concept
Lichtspielplatz is an open-source tool designed to simulate and visualize complex optical phenomena directly within the browser. Utilizing modern web technologies like WebGPU and WebAssembly (WASM), it enables real-time ray tracing and wave optics visualizations. The goal is to create an interactive platform for physics education, research, and civic engagement that surpasses the capabilities of traditional, often proprietary, desktop software.

## Core Features
*   **Real-time Ray Tracing:** Simulate the behavior of light rays through lenses, mirrors, prisms, and other optical elements with instant visual feedback.
*   **Wave Optics Visualization:** Explore phenomena like diffraction and interference through interactive wavefront simulations.
*   **Interactive Scene Creation:** Users can drag-and-drop optical components, adjust their properties (focal length, refractive index, shape), and define light sources.
*   **Data Output and Analysis:** Ability to export simulation results or measure parameters such as focal point, intensity, or aberrations.
*   **WebGPU Acceleration:** Leverages the GPU for high-performance computations and high-resolution visualizations.

## Use Cases and Target Audiences
*   **Universities and Schools (TU Berlin, educational institutions):** A powerful and accessible tool for physics labs and lectures, making abstract concepts tangible. Allows students to perform virtual experiments that would otherwise require expensive equipment.
*   **Research (DFG):** Prototyping and verification of optical setups, rapid iteration in the development of new optical systems, or analysis of complex light paths.
*   **Municipalities (Berlin Senate Department for Urban Development):** Analysis of light pollution in urban areas, optimization of city lighting, assessment of solar potential on building surfaces by simulating sun exposure at different times of day and year. This promotes sustainable urban planning and environmental awareness.
*   **Public:** An intuitive tool for visualizing and understanding physical principles, sparking curiosity and promoting STEM education.

## Uniqueness
Unlike existing solutions that often function as black-box software, Lichtspielplatz offers a transparent, open-source, and browser-based platform that utilizes cutting-edge web technologies to make complex optics simulations accessible to a broad user base. Its potential integration with real-world urban data (e.g., Berlin's 3D city models) would create unique added value for urban planning.
